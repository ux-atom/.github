## What this organization is

This is where we build. Product and client work lives here, mostly in private repositories. Our open-source work and public write-ups live at **[github.com/uxatomhq](https://github.com/uxatomhq)**.

## What we build

Complete products, not parts of them. One team designs, builds, deploys, and then stays on to run what we built.

- **Web and mobile products**: Vue, Nuxt, React, TypeScript
- **Business systems**: HRM, POS and supershop, e-commerce
- **Cloud and infrastructure**: deployment, monitoring, maintenance
- **Desktop and device software**: Rust, Tauri, Swift, embedded

## How we work

Discover, Design, Develop, Deploy, Manage, Scale. Every phase ends with something usable. The last two have no end date, because that is where systems actually live.
